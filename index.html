FY2026 media performance and strategy audit

What I found

  • AI Max campaigns returned 0.41x, well below manual campaigns.
  • 20% of Meta budget sat in 19 campaigns that produced zero leads.
  • Short courses took 61% of spend for the weakest segment return, while micro courses converted best at 23.6%.

What I did

Joined Google and Meta spend to Salesforce pipeline data and Clarity UX data, ranked creatives by volume and cost, and checked what Meta contributed beyond last-click (it assisted 40% of short course journeys).

Recommended

End AI Max and rebuild as manual search campaigns, pause the zero-lead Meta campaigns, move 15% of short course budget to online qualification PMax, and run an 8-week creative refresh cycle.

Campaign landing page optimisation

What I found

Paid media drove a traffic spike (519 sessions in one day), but 79% of visitors bounced. Targeting was fine. The page was the problem: mobile users tapped a hero video that did nothing, and 16% of tracked sessions were bots.

What I did

Used Clarity heatmaps, dead and rage tap data and render times to write a fix list in plain steps for the Strapi team: swap the video for a static image, compress banner images (5.05s average render), and stop non-clickable text looking clickable.

Outcome

Of 62 high-intent visitors in the window, 45 were on mobile and 37% went on to submit an application.
